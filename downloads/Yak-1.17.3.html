Yak 1.17.3

New: History & Words, one window for your past dictations and everything Yak has learned. Click any word Yak got wrong, type the right one, and it is fixed from then on. Click the word next to it to combine two words. It replaces Dictation History, Review & Improve and the word boxes in Settings. Also new: spell out a code such as a VIN or a confirmation number one character at a time and Yak writes it as a single piece.
