Yak 1.8.1

Two additions to Translate mode: hold fn+Shift to flip a single dictation (English for one message in a translate app, translated anywhere else), and a one-time What's New card that introduces the feature after you update. Teaching also talks back now — correct a dictation in History and Yak tells you exactly what it learned, marks the entry Taught, and pins names into your vocabulary so they stay fixed even when they're misheard differently next time.
