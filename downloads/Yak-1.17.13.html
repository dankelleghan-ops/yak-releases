What changed: the cleanup model stays warm while you talk. Transcription runs on whichever chip your Mac is fastest with. Its one-time setup now happens when Yak opens, so your first dictation of the day is quick too. Recordings longer than 60 seconds are split at pauses, which fixes a block of speech going missing past about 90 seconds.

Measured on one Apple Silicon Mac with 20 real dictations. Your Mac's numbers may differ a little.
