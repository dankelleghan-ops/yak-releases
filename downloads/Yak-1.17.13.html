Yak 1.17.13

Dictation comes back faster after you let go of fn, with the same words as before. The cleanup model is kept warm while you talk, so longer dictations are cleaned about half a second sooner. Transcription now runs on whichever chip your Mac is fastest with, which trims every dictation by about a third, and its one-time setup happens when Yak launches instead of on your first dictation of the day. Paragraphs within one dictation are cleaned one after another, which is also quicker. Recordings longer than 60 seconds are split at pauses, which fixes a block of speech going missing past about 90 seconds.
