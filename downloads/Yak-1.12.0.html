Yak 1.12.0

Pick which microphone Yak records from, in Settings → Cleanup. Yak uses it whatever macOS is doing, so AirPods can stay your default for listening — Bluetooth mics wake slowly and were losing the first words of a sentence. If the mic is ever slow to wake, the pill now says so. Also: dictations into Reminders and other task apps no longer arrive with a full stop.
