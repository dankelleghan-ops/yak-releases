Yak 1.9.2

Chat messages no longer end in a period. In Messages, WhatsApp and similar apps, "Never stops." now pastes as "Never stops" — a trailing period reads as curt in texting. Only the final period goes: question marks, exclamation points, mid-message periods and abbreviations like etc. all stay, and email, documents and Claude are never touched. There's a switch in Settings → Cleanup if you'd rather keep it.
