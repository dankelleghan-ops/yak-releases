Yak 1.6.6

New troubleshooting option, off by default. Turn on "Keep recent recordings" in Settings under Debug and Yak saves your last 20 dictations to your Mac — the audio, plus what it heard and what it pasted. That makes it possible to tell whether a wrong word came from the transcription or the cleanup, which otherwise looks identical. Nothing is uploaded, and clearing your history deletes the recordings too.
