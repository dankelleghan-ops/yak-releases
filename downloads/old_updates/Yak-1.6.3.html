Yak 1.6.3

Better handling of spoken punctuation when you're using Apple Intelligence for cleanup. Saying "question mark" or "period" now reliably becomes the mark itself instead of occasionally leaving the words behind, and Yak knows the difference between the command and the ordinary word ("a period of quiet" stays as written). Also better at applying it when you correct yourself mid-sentence — "Tuesday, no actually Wednesday" now comes out as just Wednesday, without rewording the rest of your sentence.
