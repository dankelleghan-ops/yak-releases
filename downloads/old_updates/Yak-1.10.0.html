Yak 1.10.0

Ask for a tone out loud: end a dictation with "make this sound professional", "make it more concise" or "make this friendlier" and Yak rewrites that one dictation in that register. The instruction is removed before anything else runs, so it never lands in your message, and it only counts when it is the whole last thing you say. Facts, names, numbers and questions are never changed.
