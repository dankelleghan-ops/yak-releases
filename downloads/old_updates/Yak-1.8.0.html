Yak 1.8.0

New: Translate mode — dictate in English and your message pastes in another language (Thai, Spanish, Japanese, and 18 more). Turn it on in Settings → Translate, pick which apps it applies to (Messages and WhatsApp out of the box), and everywhere else Yak behaves exactly as before. A speaker setting gets Thai politeness particles (ครับ/ค่ะ) right for whoever is dictating. If a translation ever fails, Yak copies your English to the clipboard instead of pasting the wrong language.
