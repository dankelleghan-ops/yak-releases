Yak 1.17.11

Dropping words into the middle of a sentence now fits the text around it: no stray capital, period or line break, and a missing space is added, including when you highlight words to replace them and in Mail. The on-device cleanup can no longer drop your last word or answer a question you dictated. 'gotta' stays 'gotta' in Messages and other chat apps. Dates like 'the nineteenth and twentieth of November' become 'the 19th and 20th'. An email that opens with just a name ('Heather, we...') gets the greeting on its own line. No stray capital after 'p.m.', and a spoken 'exclamation point' that the speech engine hears as 'exclaim' now works. A name that only looks a little like one of your saved words is no longer swapped for it.
