Yak 1.7.0

New: Review & Improve, in the menu. Yak has always been able to learn from your corrections, but there was no way to see whether anything it learned was actually being used. Now you can — every word fix shows how many times it has really changed a transcript, and ones that have never fired get flagged so you can throw them out. Recent dictations are listed there too, so you can correct anything it got wrong in one pass. It'll mention when there's enough new material to be worth a look.
